How to Return Your Car

Returning your rental car is simple! Follow these five steps to complete your rental.

01

Return Your Car

Drive your rental vehicle to the designated car return point at Ngee Ann Polytechnic on your scheduled return date.

02

Vehicle Inspection

Our AZoom employee will inspect the returned vehicle to check its condition and identify any damages.

03

Inspection Results

The employee will update the vehicle's inspection status and record any damage charges if damages are found.

04

Complete Payment

The final rental amount, including any applicable damage charges, will be charged to the credit card provided during booking.
